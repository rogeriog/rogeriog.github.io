SOAP compression schemes — off · crossover · mu1nu1 · mu2

The uncompressed SOAP power spectrum pairs every species-resolved radial channel with every other: with S species and nmax=4 there are S·4 channels and the upper triangle of all their products is stored — length grows like S². Darby et al.'s mu1nu1 (implemented in dscribe as compression={"mode":"mu1nu1"}) replaces the second factor with a sum over all species:

pZ₁,Z₂nn′l = Σm (cZ₁nlm)* · (Σz czn′lm)

One species index survives, the pair structure collapses, and the length scales linearly with S.

Real dscribe anchors at S=3: off 390 · mu1nu1 240 · crossover 150 · mu2 50. At S=20 (MP-20): off 16 200 · mu1nu1 1 600 · crossover 2 000 · mu2 50. At S=89 (full MP): off 317 730 · mu1nu1 7 120 · crossover 8 950 · mu2 50.

What each scheme keeps: off keeps everything (species pairs); mu1nu1 keeps species identity on one side but sums the other over species; crossover keeps per-species blocks but drops cross-species terms; mu2 is element-agnostic. Accuracy loss is usually modest versus the dimensional savings.
