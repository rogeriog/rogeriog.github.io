About

Hello, I'm Rogério — a soon-to-be PhD in Materials Science. My academic work focuses on using physics and machine learning to discover insights in energy materials, with publications spanning halide perovskites, oxide nanostructures, and ceramic processing.

I'm enthusiastic about programming, data science, and AI, and I'm dedicated to improving productivity while exploring fresh horizons across my academic work and development projects.

Beyond research, I build tools: automation, autonomous agents, and small systems that make everyday work faster — including this site, which is maintained by an OpenClaw agent fleet I supervise over Telegram.
